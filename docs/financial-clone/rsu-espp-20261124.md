## Behavior and scope

RSU delivery separates wage value, federal withholding, entered other withholding and an assumed incremental federal-tax gap. The 2026 optional supplemental method applies 22% to remaining annual $1 million capacity and 37% to the excess, subject to the documented eligibility assumptions. Actual federal withholding can be entered instead. Other withholding is a supplied dollar amount, not an automatic FICA/state calculation. Retained shares are a fractional planning equivalent, not broker execution. The sale mode uses the already-taxed income-date basis once and preserves capital losses without inventing a loss tax benefit.

ESPP supports a confirmed Section 423 lot sold in 2026, using known purchase/grant-date-as-if prices or a 0–15% discount formula. Compensation income is added to basis once. Disqualifying compensation is not capped at sale profit, so a wage component can coexist with a capital loss. Disposition and capital-term tests remain separate. Calendar anniversaries, leap-day clamping and exact-boundary cautions are explicit. Unknown/nonqualified plans stop with a repairable error. Employment/plan qualification, the $25,000 accrual rule, full returns, state tax, wash sales and special deferred awards are outside scope. Entered marginal rates are scenarios, not a tax-bracket engine.

Both tools join category directories, the financial directory, relevant existing tools, guides, glossary and curated llms navigation. Real catalog destinations are used, including `/tax-calculator`. No separate pages were created merely for keyword synonyms.
