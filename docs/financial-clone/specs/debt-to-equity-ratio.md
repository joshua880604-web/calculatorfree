# Debt-to-Equity Ratio Calculator - Definitions & Changes

A manual book-equity leverage worksheet, using either total liabilities or interest-bearing debt. Nonpositive equity is flagged. Scenarios separate repayment from existing cash and new equity retained as cash; cash availability, covenants and credit approval are outside the model.

The executable model lives at src/lib/calculators/debt-to-equity-ratio.ts. All reference input fields and examples are frozen in evidence/ratio-property-three-reference-inputs-20261118.json; independent Decimal expectations are generated by scripts/check-ratio-property-three-reference.py. Concepts and exclusions are explained in the calculation page, comparison guide and term.

Source: https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/entrepreneur-toolkit/financial-tools/debt-to-equity-ratio
