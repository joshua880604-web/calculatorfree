# Gross Rent Multiplier Calculator - GRM, Price & Rent

Whole-property price and gross scheduled rent before deductions. Reverse modes use a multiplier chosen by you. No net yield, cap-rate calculation, appraisal, guaranteed payback period, market rent or recommendation.

The executable model lives at src/lib/calculators/gross-rent-multiplier.ts. All reference input fields and examples are frozen in evidence/ratio-property-three-reference-inputs-20261118.json; independent Decimal expectations are generated by scripts/check-ratio-property-three-reference.py. Concepts and exclusions are explained in the calculation page, comparison guide and term.

Source: https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/real-estate/commercial-term-lending/what-is-a-gross-rent-multiplier-grm

No competitor interface or live quote parity is claimed.
